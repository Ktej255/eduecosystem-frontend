## 💰 Estimated Costs (Monthly)

### Minimum (Budget Option)
- **Hosting**: Railway ($5-20) or DigitalOcean ($12)
- **Database**: Included with hosting
- **Domain**: GoDaddy/Namecheap ($10/year = $1/month)
- **Monitoring**: Sentry free tier, UptimeRobot free tier
- **Total**: **~$15-25/month**

### Recommended (Production-Ready)
- **Frontend**: Vercel ($20/month or free hobby tier)
- **Backend**: Railway ($20-50/month)
- **Database**: Railway PostgreSQL ($10/month)
- **Storage**: AWS S3 ($5-15/month)
- **CDN**: Cloudflare (free)
- **Monitoring**: Sentry ($26/month) + Uptime Robot free tier
- **Total**: **~$80-120/month**

### Enterprise (High Traffic)
- **Hosting**: AWS/GCP ($200-500/month)
- **Database**: RDS ($50-200/month)
- **CDN**: Cloudflare Pro ($20/month)
- **Monitoring**: Datadog ($15/host/month)
- **Total**: **~$300-800/month**
