The Divine Trinity

Brahma, Vishnu, and Siva make the Triad in Hinduism. Their power is vested in their Sakthis. Without their Sakthis, the Gods are powerless. Brahma and Sarasvati, Vishnu and Sri, & Siva and Sakthi are the power couples. The Sakthi is always red. The Gods are portrayed with upturned triangles, while their Sakthis are inverted triangles, based on the escutcheon of the respective genders.
