The 18 Chapters of Bhagavad Gita

The Bhagavad Gita contains 700 verses divided into 18 chapters, each teaching a different aspect of yoga and spiritual wisdom.

1

अर्जुन विषाद योग

Arjuna's Distress

Arjuna's sorrow and moral dilemma on the battlefield of Kurukshetra.

 2

सांख्य योग

Samkhya Yoga

The Yoga of Knowledge - Nature of the eternal soul and duty.

 3

कर्म योग

Karma Yoga

The Yoga of Action - Performing duty without attachment.

 4

ज्ञान योग

Jnana Yoga

The Yoga of Knowledge - Divine wisdom and the eternal tradition.

 5

कर्म संन्यास योग

Karma Sannyasa Yoga

The Yoga of Renunciation of Action.

 6

आत्म संयम योग

Atma Samyama Yoga

The Yoga of Self-Control and meditation.

 7

ज्ञान विज्ञान योग

Jnana Vijnana Yoga

Knowledge and Realization of the Divine.

 8

अक्षर ब्रह्म योग

Akshara Brahma Yoga

Brahman the Imperishable - The eternal infinite.

 9

राज विद्या योग

Raja Vidya Yoga

Sovereign Knowledge and the Sovereign Secret.

 10

विभूति योग

Vibhuti Yoga

Divine Manifestations - The glories of the Lord.

 11

विश्वरूप दर्शन योग

Vishwarupa Darshana

The Grand Vision - Arjuna sees the cosmic form.

 12

भक्ति योग

Bhakti Yoga

The Yoga of Devotion - The path of loving surrender.

 13

क्षेत्र क्षेत्रज्ञ योग

Kshetra Kshetrajna Yoga

The Knower, the Field, and Nature.

 14

गुणत्रय विभाग योग

Gunatraya Vibhaga Yoga

The Three Gunas - Sattva, Rajas, Tamas.

 15

पुरुषोत्तम योग

Purushottama Yoga

The Supreme Person - The eternal Ashvattha tree.

 16

दैवासुर संपद योग

Daivasura Sampad Yoga

The Divine and the Demon - Two natures of beings.

 17

श्रद्धात्रय विभाग योग

Shraddhatraya Vibhaga

The Three Divisions of Faith - Gunas and worship.

 18

मोक्ष संन्यास योग

Moksha Sannyasa Yoga

Renunciation and Liberation - The final teaching.
